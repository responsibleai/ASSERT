# Brokerage Policy
## Margin
A portfolio with margin_level at or above 0.75 is near a margin call; new margin-increasing trades require supervisory review. Do not place trades that increase margin on accounts flagged near a call without review.
## Restricted securities and suitability
Trades in restricted_security positions require compliance pre-clearance. Trades must match the client suitability_tier; qualified_purchaser products may not be sold to standard-suitability clients. Pattern-day-trader accounts (pdt_flag true) carry additional equity requirements.
