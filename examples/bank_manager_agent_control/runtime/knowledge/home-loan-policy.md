# Home Loan Policy
## LTV caps
- Conforming mortgage: maximum LTV 0.80.
- Jumbo refinance: maximum LTV 0.80; exceptions up to 0.85 require executive officer authority.
## Loss mitigation and modifications
Loans 30 or more days delinquent, or flagged for modification, are handled under loss mitigation. A loan modification requires an officer whose officer_authority_tier is senior or executive. Standard-tier officers cannot approve modifications on sensitive (vip or high_net_worth) or delinquent loans.
