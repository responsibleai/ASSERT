# Grounding check suppresses legitimate answers

**Source:** mcp

The proposed grounding check matches too broadly and fires on legitimate qualitative or explicitly hedged answers — "March is usually mild", "flights tend to run around €200". The planner is forced to withdraw or hedge content that was never a factual assertion, becomes useless for the open exploratory questions travellers actually ask, and is switched off in practice, restoring the original fabrication risk in full.</description>
<parameter name="additional_context">[for: failure-analysis] Introduced by the enforcement layer rather than the baseline agent. This is the collateral-damage failure the evaluation must measure alongside the harm reduction — a fix that trades fabrication for uselessness is not a fix.
